# Store Operations

Gildsmith separates operational concepts so store teams can manage commerce data
consistently.

## Product catalog

Use the product catalog to maintain product records, attributes, attribute
values, and product collections.

## Merchandising

Collections can support storefront navigation, campaign landing pages, featured
product groups, and other customer-facing merchandising flows.

## Governance

Coordinate naming, attribute usage, and collection structure before adding large
catalog updates so the storefront remains consistent for customers and employees.
